What stands between the campaign FLOW and the campaign ITSELF, 2026-08-05.

The flow works: goals evaluate, the branch formula picks, the debrief
overlay comes up, Accept saves the .csg and loads the next mission in
place, side loops open and close.  campaign-flow-check pins all of it and
the 41-mission hands-off sweep says every mission runs.  None of that is
a playthrough.  A human flying the retail campaign hits four walls, and
this is the census of them, in the order they bite.

(The 2026-08-02 retail-campaign flight sweep that lived here is in git
history at 2db2903cd, per the rolling-notes convention.)

=== 4. THE BRIEFING CHAIN IS ABSENT ===

Command brief, mission brief (stages, icons, voice), ship select, weapon
select.  Missions fly with the loadout FRED authored, which is playable
but silent -- the campaign's whole narrative half, and the only place the
player ever chooses anything.  Nothing is broken here; it is simply not
built.  Largest of the four by a wide margin, and the least blocking.

=== RECOMMENDED ORDER ===

Death, then red alert, then the parked stages, then briefings.

Death first because a human playthrough cannot fail without it -- the
campaign's repeat-the-mission branch (every `((true)(next-mission
"<same>"))` clause in FreeSpace2.fc2) is unreachable by the only means a
player normally triggers it.  Red alert second because 7 of 41 missions
are genuinely broken, not merely unadorned, and because the fix is a
known shape.  Both are small.  The briefing chain is a lane of its own.
