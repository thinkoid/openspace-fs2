linklist.hh assessment — 2026-07-28

Overall: the intrusive circular doubly-linked ring is an appropriate design for
FS2's fixed pools.  A sentinel ring makes insertion/removal branch-free and
removal head-independent; intrusive links avoid allocation.  The thin head also
removed substantial fake payload (documented ship shrink: 1744 -> 1352 bytes)
and the template API caught real retail macro misuse.  This is not suspect merely
because it differs from std::list; std::list also uses a links-only sentinel ring.
